About

I am a Sr. GRC & AI Governance Analyst with 11+ years of Information Security and GRC experience across regulated fintech and healthcare environments. I spent seven years in policy management, maintaining a repository of 25+ policies, standards, plans, and related Information Security documentation through an annual review cycle. For the past two years I've served as primary audit liaison for PCI DSS, SOX 404, SOC 1, and SOC 2 examinations, gathering and validating the control evidence they depend on.

Today, I apply that foundation directly to AI governance through a practicum with Technology Roles by Indigomark. That work has included evaluating three AI-enabled tools across six risk domains (privacy, accuracy, bias, security, vendor, and regulatory), scoring likelihood against impact, and building an acceptable use policy and tool and vendor evaluation checklist to remediate the high-risk findings. I ground this work in the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and the approach holds across engagements: find where the real exposure sits, then build controls a team can operate day to day.

I am currently pursuing the IAPP AIGP certification, formalizing the AI governance skill set that frameworks like ISO/IEC 42001 are built around.
